# Military history

There is very much military history content online. These are some resources that I find interesting and useful.

## Websites

If you have an interest in WW2 history, I recommend the
site [Project'44 the road to liberation](https://www.project44.ca/). Their [interactive
WWII web maps](https://map.project44.ca/) and [story maps](https://map.project44.ca/story-maps) are excellent.

Dr. Leo Niehorster's site [World War II Armed Forces — Orders of Battle and
Organizations](http://niehorster.org/) is also very interesting. The site gives very much data.

[HyperWar, A hypertext history of the second world war](https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/) has a large collection of
documents, maps, photographs and other material about the war. You can browse the resources by country, by theater of
war, or by service branch.

[U-boat Archive](https://www.uboatarchive.net/) is an excellent resource about submarine warfare in WW2. The site has
much data. It includes U-boat war diaries with related KMZ files, charts and photographs.

Do you want to know more about the battleship Bismarck and the Kriegsmarine? Then look
at [KBismarck.com](https://www.kbismarck.com/).

If you have an interest in Cold War history, look at Hans Boersma's
site [Netherlands Armed Forces ORDER OF BATTLE 1985](https://www.orbat85.nl/). It is very good. The site gives data
about the orders of battle, the equipment and the organization of the NATO and Warsaw Pact armed forces in the Cold
War.

[Naval-History.net](https://www.naval-history.net/index.htm) gives much data about the history of the Royal Navy. The
site also has an online version of Gordon Smith's
book [BATTLE ATLAS of the FALKLANDS WAR 1982 by Land, Sea and Air](https://www.naval-history.net/NAVAL1982FALKLANDS.htm).
This book is one of the sources for the main demo scenario of ORBAT Mapper.

The [Battle Order](https://www.battleorder.org/) website has much detailed content about ORBATs, TO&Es, tactics,
logistics and more.

## YouTube

- [Battle Order](https://www.youtube.com/@BattleOrder) - military subjects. These include history, the organization and
  the equipment of military units, and military doctrine
- [DigitalBattlefieldTours](https://www.youtube.com/@DigitalBattlefieldTours) - a full battlefield tour that you can
  see at home
- [Historigraph](https://www.youtube.com/@historigraph) - animated historical documentaries
- [Mark Felton Productions](https://www.youtube.com/@MarkFeltonProductions) - many different historical subjects,
  primarily about WWII and the Cold War
- [The Great War](https://www.youtube.com/@TheGreatWarSeries)
- [Sabaton History](https://www.youtube.com/@SabatonHistory) - the historical background of all Sabaton songs
- [Covert Cabal](https://www.youtube.com/@CovertCabal) - military analysis
- [Military History Visualized](https://www.youtube.com/@MilitaryHistoryVisualized) - military history from classical
  times to current conflicts
- [Eastory](https://www.youtube.com/@Eastory) - a history channel, primarily about Eastern Europe and military history
